THE PREMISE

Before the flood, before the ark, a man walked with God for 300 years. His name was Enoch. His story survived in a book most people never open.

ENOCHTU is the animated telling of that walk — from an empty village to a city that forgot the sky. Seven episodes. One journey from the storm to the sunrise.

This is the story the music has been telling all along. The albums are the soundtrack. The anime is the world.
